**Tibo Sottiaux (@thsottiaux) is OpenAI’s highly public, community-facing product/engineering lead** — the guy who ships Codex and ChatGPT agent products, talks in public about compute and rate limits, and became a meme as the “reset guy.”

He is a natural Broforce / Expendabros character: a clean-cut European operator who keeps the lab’s agents online, dumps extra ammo on the squad, then tells everyone to burn it.

## Identity and background

- Full name: Thibault “Tibo” Sottiaux. Belgian. Applied math / CS background (UCLouvain). French/Dutch/English speaker.
- Path: Google Maps → Google DeepMind research infra (including work around AlphaGo-era tooling) → OpenAI in 2024. Built/led Codex, then took a much bigger remit.
- Current public role (as of 2026): Head of core products under Greg Brockman. Scope includes ChatGPT, Codex, API, agent infra, enterprise / ChatGPT Work. Tasked with turning ChatGPT into a more proactive “super app” / personal agent.
- Look: Dark hair, clean-shaven or light stubble, big smile, black crewneck. Approachable “staff engineer who accidentally became a celebrity” energy rather than Sam-Altman-keynote energy.

## Public persona

Tibo’s X account is not a distant exec feed. It is a **shipping + customer-support + hype** account.

Core traits:

- **The Reset King.** His defining meme is announcing usage-quota resets for Codex / ChatGPT paid users after outages, quality bugs, demand spikes, or just because he promised Tuesday. Community calls extra refills “Tibo resets.” He leans into it.
- **Always shipping.** Constant product drops: Agents API, computer use, plugins, Codex app features, model retirements, Windows alpha, site hosting, Git AI acquisition/welcome posts. Tone is “there is so much good stuff it is a bit ridiculous.”
- **Compute realist.** Talks plainly about demand outrunning supply, scarce inference, efficiency as the real moat, pausing $200 Pro signups to protect existing users, 5-hour limits to smooth load. Not mystical AGI-priest energy. Ops + product energy.
- **Community operator.** Replies to users, credits people who filed examples, says “always grateful for this incredible community,” asks “what should we ship next week?”, posts at 3am on a Tuesday.
- **Dry, slightly theatrical hype.** He does not tweet like a VC. He tweets like a racetrack announcer who also runs the cluster:
  - “Ladies and gentlemen... start... your... ENGINES.”
  - “Burn those tokens”
  - “Reset all propagated. Sweet dreams.”
  - “👁️codex👁️”
  - “3am on a tuesday”
  - “Sometimes physics can't be cheated”

He is friendly, not chaotic. He will pause plans, retire a model named GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark with a joke, then immediately tease the next drop. That mix — generous, slightly smug about shipping, honest about capacity — is the character.

## Expendabros character brief

**Codename:** Tibo / Reset / Tokenburner / Quota Slayer

**Faction:** OpenAI lab, product/platform wing

**Archetype:** The quartermaster-ace. Not the prophet. Not the safety monk. The guy who keeps the agents fed and then yells “GO.”

**Specials that write themselves:**

1. **Tibo Reset** — full squad ammo, cooldowns, and token meter refill. Screen text: “RESET ALL PROPAGATED.”
2. **Burn Those Tokens** — dump the entire meter into a ridiculous firehose. High DPS, leaves you empty.
3. **Start Your Engines** — temporary speed + fire-rate buff. Race-announcer VO.
4. **Astra Cook** — summon a coding-agent drone that auto-hacks terminals / opens doors / writes a workaround mid-fight.
5. **Physics Can’t Be Cheated** — anti-cheat / anti-glitch line. Maybe a hard cap that prevents infinite ammo exploits.

**Catchphrases for VO:**

- “Ladies and gentlemen… start your engines.”
- “Burn those tokens.”
- “See you soon.”
- “Sweet dreams.”
- “Always be shipping.”
- “Priority is existing users.”
- “There is never a boring day working in the open.”

**Visual gag:** Black tee, headset or laptop slung like a rifle, a giant “USAGE” meter on his back that he slaps to refill the team. When he ults, rate-limit UI explodes into confetti.

**How he plays vs other AI-lab bros:**

- Altman / Brockman types: vision and lab politics.
- Safety leads: brakes and red tape.
- Research leads: new model drops.
- **Tibo: live-site reliability, agent harness, user delight, and the dopamine of extra quota.** He is the reason the run doesn’t end when the token bar hits zero.

That is the usable persona: a smiling Belgian shipper who treats rate limits like an action-movie ammo crate and the developer community like his squad.
