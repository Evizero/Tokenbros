# Marcus: a volley worth recalling

Marcus's shared cartridge stock is four instead of three. The small primary cartridges keep their existing launch cadence, speed, damage and spin, but remain outbound for up to five seconds instead of 0.72, with up to 16 ricochets. At the ricochet limit they return instead of vanishing. Map edges also bank them back into play.

E still recalls the volley immediately. Returning cartridges receive six seconds to reach Marcus from the extended range; repeat recalls do not renew that timer. Each projectile still damages a target once outbound and once on return. Batch echoes cost no extra slots, and the other game cartridges still reserve one shared slot. HUD stock displays four.

Validation covers the four-shot cap, distant flight and return, more than eight ricochets, boundary banking, return damage/refill, batch echoes, lifecycle cleanup, Invader and Pac-Man regressions and roster previews. Batch's preview now finishes after its recalled volley lands even if the dodge cooldown already expired. Local changes only.
