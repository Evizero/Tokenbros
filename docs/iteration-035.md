# Character movement on Space

Local gameplay revision, September 2026. The user chose claw grappling for Peter, levitation for Pidalf, token-burning rocket boots for Tibo, and moving Marcus's existing LCD launch off the weapon selector. Dimillian keeps his existing spaceship flight; no new movement design was chosen for his other forms.

- **Marcus:** Space starts an ordinary jump immediately. A fresh airborne Space press, held for 320ms, starts the existing LCD charge; aim with the mouse and release to launch. Holding the original ground-jump press never starts LCD. Landing before the hold threshold disarms it. The air charge freezes falling. The existing 0.58-second charge, 135–325px aimed launch, speed, discrete animation, impact damage, terrain collision, lingering silhouette and one-launch-per-landing rule are retained. Scroll selects only cartridges, Invader and Pac-Man (1/2/3). LCD works with any cartridge and scrolling does not cancel its charge. Art/projectile mode IDs stay stable internally as 0/2/3.
- **Peter:** jump and keep Space held for 180ms to shoot a claw at the mouse. It travels up to 450px, attaches to the first solid surface, and reels him toward it while held. Release detaches and preserves pull momentum. Terrain still blocks the player. A destroyed anchor or newly obstructed rope breaks the connection. A miss expires and requires another press. The claw and segmented tether are drawn in world space.
- **Pidalf:** keep Space held after jumping to levitate for up to 2.6 seconds per landing. It holds altitude with neutral input. W/Up rises and S/Down descends at up to 320px/s, with matching 320px/s horizontal steering. Releasing W/S immediately holds the new altitude. Levitation steering does not grab nearby ladders or cables. Releasing ends lift; pressing again uses the remaining time. Landing restores the reserve. One leg folds back while the other hangs, with rising force wisps around his body. A gentle 2.5px visual bob moves the entire pose and its wisps on a roughly 1.85-second cycle without changing the physical body or aim. There is no ring or floor-like effect beneath his feet; the airtime meter sits vertically beside him.
- **Tibo:** keep the initial jump press held for 350ms to fire rocket boots. A fresh airborne Space press ignites immediately, without the grounded hold threshold; a ground tap remains an ordinary jump. Thrust eases toward 285px/s upward and consumes 150 tokens/second from the same 1,000-token pool as the gun. Releasing stops spending immediately; an empty pool cuts the boots. Foot flames and a cumulative token-spend readout make the cost visible. Existing reset and token-recovery abilities still restore this shared resource.

`src/vertical.ts` owns movement input, lift resources, grapple attachment and rendering. Shared movement still resolves collision via `World.move`. Player lifecycle cleanup cancels movement on pause, death and restart. The movement state travels in co-op actor snapshots.

The roster includes Space previews for boots, grappling, levitation and LCD, using the real movement implementation. The controls ribbon identifies each character's Space action. `scripts/playtest-vertical.js` checks ascent, resource drain, collision, release, finite airtime, anchor removal, misses, cartridge selection and lifecycle cleanup. Existing LCD, Invader and Pac-Man checks cover retained behavior.
