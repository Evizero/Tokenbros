# Iteration 027 — situational hero speech bubbles

User requested catchphrases for spawning, dying, locations and interesting events, then explicitly chose **speech bubbles only**. This overrides the initial spoken-dialogue proposal. No speech synthesis, voice clips, voice toggle or changes to enemy robot audio were added.

`src/hero-lines.json` is the editable dialogue bank for all three bros. Lines are fictional game dialogue inspired by their established character themes, not claims of authentic quotations. Tibo uses quota/reset/operator humor, Peter addresses his claws and pack, and Dimillian uses French flourishes and game-development jokes.

Triggers include spawn, death, respawn, low health, three kills within 1.6 seconds, signature abilities and successful ability outcomes. Tibo reacts to an empty gun, reset impact, throwing/catching his double and a meaningful shield conversion. Peter reacts to deployment, Molt and Prism. Dimillian reacts to connected combo finishers, polymorph, successful pairing and each form's ultimate. One-time location lines cover the tower, cable ride and boss arrival; uplinks, rescues and mission victory also have reactions. Victory uses a named quote in the result panel so the overlay does not hide it.

`HeroBarks` keeps a single character-anchored bubble, short wrapped text, small tail and fade. The initial name/accent styling was replaced by a pixel comic balloon in iteration 028 following user feedback. Death stays anchored to its original location while other bubbles follow the player. Canvas text has a matching screen-reader live region. The normal chatter gap is seven seconds, most events have a 25-second repeat cooldown, and variants rotate without immediate repetition. Routine blocked lines are dropped. Only one important pending reaction is kept for up to five seconds; death/victory can interrupt lower-priority dialogue. Respawn waits for a death line to finish. Pause freezes timing, restarting clears run state, and returning to the roster removes dialogue. Preview simulations produce no bubbles or live-region updates.

Validation: production build and 30 deterministic tests pass. `playtest-hero-barks.js` checks all three spawn variants, rate limiting, rotating variants, death interruption/deferred respawn, pause, actual ability triggers, multikill and low-health triggers, once-only tower entry, boss/uplink/rescue/victory, roster cleanup and preview isolation. Zero page errors. Spawn bubbles were captured for each character and inspected at `output/playwright/hero-bubble-*-v27.png`. QA is in the isolated browser.
