# Field test 029 — Pidalf

The user approved the physical manipulation kit and requested satisfying animation. The new character uses an amber accent, grey wizard cloak, tied-back hair, glasses and short beard. This supersedes the unimplemented lightning/loop-spell proposal.

## Controls and mechanics

- Click: a full-body backhand launches a traveling crescent shockwave along the aimed direction, so aiming beyond an enemy still hits it. The wave speed scales continuously from 1,100px/s at minimum influence to 360px/s at maximum influence for up to 400px and stops at terrain, damaging breakable cover. Ordinary robots disassemble; armored shield bots lose their shell first. Turrets take repeated damage. Bigger influence widens the wave and allows up to three hits, with longer recovery. Anticipation, follow-through, directional armor/debris, skeleton-collapse afterimage and mechanical impact sounds sell the strike. Shockwave quips such as “SLOP!”, “delete the nonsense.” and “too much abstraction.” use the shared speech-bubble system, with its normal cooldowns and variant rotation. The dedicated SLOP popup was removed. There is no voice audio.
- Hold E: grab bots, explosive barrels, live grenades, map crates or compacted scrap in the cursor region, within a mass budget of one to six. Acquisition ignores line of sight but keeps the range and mass limits. Bodies follow with spring-like lag and obey terrain collision; they do not phase through walls. Release E to impart bounded, smoothed momentum. Grabs expire after 3.2 seconds, then recover based on mass. Fast thrown robots damage other robots and take impact damage against floors/walls. Pause drops them without a throw. Nearby mission uplinks retain E priority. Barrels cost one mass, rotate in flight and detonate after a hard impact with terrain or a bot. Grabbing does not ignite them; an existing fuse keeps burning while held. Q and traveling shockwaves can also push barrels and live grenades. Grenade fuses keep burning while held. Wooden crate tiles become rotating physical props when grabbed or pushed by a shockwave/staff slam; moving crates can damage bots.
- Hold E + click: compact the held bot(s) without switching keys or requiring the cursor to land back on a lagging body. Held targets must be visible, and normal mass, armor and compaction cooldown rules apply. At small scale one held bot is selected; at large scale visible held bots merge. Keep E held through the squeeze to retain the resulting scrap, then yank and release E to throw it. Release E or pause during compaction to cancel automatic carrying. The squeeze pauses the hold timer and leaves at least 1.5 seconds to throw the result. F while grabbing uses the same shortcut.
- F: compact cursor targets. Below 35% influence, the cursor must directly hit a bot (with a small 4px aiming allowance) to press it into an 18px cube. At 35% and above, F can start at an empty visible point; the black hole gathers eligible bots already nearby or entering during the first 70% of its windup into a spiral and creates a rolling ball whose mass and size reflect its contents. Compaction placement and acquisition require line of sight; it cannot collect through cover. Armor is stripped once per cast before heavy enemies can be compacted. Bosses are never draggable or instantly compacted; exposed cores can take direct damage. Compaction takes 0.65–1.45 seconds and recovers over 3.5–7.5 seconds.
- Scroll: continuous influence scale; radius, capacity, recovery and staff-tip appearance change. The in-world cursor circle expands continuously, with temporary percentage/mass feedback. No three-spell mode selector or additional mana budget.
- Q — You Shall Not Pass: lift the staff overhead with both hands, gather light, then slam it down in a radial repulsion burst. Bots, scrap, grenades and hostile bullets are pushed away within a scale-dependent 105–175px radius and line of sight; bullets become friendly. Heavy bots resist the push. Code-review/PR variations on “You shall not pass” use the shared speech bubbles; there is no dedicated Q caption. The action cancels a grab, compaction or slap and has a 4.5-second cooldown.

Compacted objects bounce, roll, hit bots and barrels, can be grabbed again and can be broken into damaging scrap by SLOP. Compacted scrap lasts up to 24 seconds, with six retained when a new compacted prop is created; detached crates last up to 60 seconds. Terrain collision is solid; actor damage is physical overlap, rather than a full rigid-body solver. Bodies are not new player platforms. Large groups are heavier to drag. Heavy-enemy resistance and bounded hold time keep normal encounters active.

## Presentation

Code-native sprite in `src/pidalf-art.ts`: planted feet, torso rotation, shoulder/hand anticipation and follow-through, trailing cloak, palm-forward force pose, pi staff in the opposite hand. `src/pidalf.ts` owns mechanics and world effects. Compaction drives a continuous reach, arm draw-back, finger curl and fist clench in the hero sprite, followed by a 240ms full-body crunch recoil and recovery. Palm motes and the target squeeze share the action timing. The reaching arm stays behind the torso. Levitation uses three very faint moving directional dashes, rising motes and velocity streaks; the previous continuous line and elliptical orbit ring are removed. Small compaction uses closing press jaws; large compaction uses inward spirals and a dark core/accretion ring. Light motion effects remain when screen shake is off; impact pauses and shake follow the existing effects preference.

The reaching arm is drawn behind the torso, so its shoulder no longer overlays the front of the chest. Torso shading puts the light inner shirt toward the aiming hand and the darker outer cloak behind him, mirrored for either facing. The face keeps round glasses, tied-back hair and a short beard. A faint influence circle and target brackets replace the prominent aiming line; with separate grab/compact feedback: E can grab through cover while F is marked blocked.

Fourth playable roster entry, two remaining placeholders, three isolated combat-reel chapters and generated entrance cutout. [Portrait prompts and provenance](research/pidalf-image-generation.md). The user's follow-up requested actual transparency; v2 replaces v1 in both entrance and roster. A small speech-bubble line bank covers spawn/death/milestones.

## Validation

`scripts/playtest-pidalf.js` exercises travel time, aiming past enemies, ordinary and armored targets, aim/cover, staff repulsion and bullet reflection, force lift/release velocity, maximum hold, low/high compaction, re-grabbing cubes, ball collision damage, pause/death/respawn cleanup and uplink operation. Barrel checks cover targeting, lift without ignition, throw momentum, impact detonation and explosions while held. It also samples the portrait's alpha. All-character roster/showcase tests now include Pidalf and two unavailable slots. Existing automated checks do not establish subjective game feel; that remains for player feedback.
